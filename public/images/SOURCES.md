# KOKO MARKET image sources

These are real Nigerian food and market photographs. They are downloaded into
`public/images` so production pages use stable first-party asset paths instead
of hotlinking image hosts. No AI-generated product photography is used.

- `nigeria-rice-beans-market.jpg` — [Bags and wheels of rice and beans](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bags_and_wheels_of_rice_and_beans.jpg), Daniel Paullll, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `rice-market.jpg` — [Rice market](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rice_market.jpg), Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `nigeria-beans-market.jpg` — [Nigeria beans](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nigeria_beans.jpg), Antoshananarivo, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `garri-seller-ilorin.jpg` — [Garri seller truck in Ilorin](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Garri_seller_truck_in_Ilorin.jpg), Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA.
- `yellow-garri.jpg` — [Yellow garri](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yellow_garri.jpg), Judethedeus, CC0.
- `plantain-market.jpg` — [Plantain Seller at the Market](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plantain_Seller_at_the_Market.jpg), Johnnybam, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `palm-oil-bottles.jpg` — [Bottled Palm-oil](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bottled_Palm-oil.jpg), Ei'eke, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `jollof-rice-plantain.jpg` — [Jollof rice and plantain](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jollof_rice_and_plantain.jpg), Wikimedia Commons, Nigerian food photograph.
- `nigerian-jollof-restaurant.jpg` — [Jollof Rice and Chicken](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jollof_Rice_and_Chicken.jpg), Count Zugu, CC BY-SA 4.0.

- `nigerian-dried-pepper.jpg` — [Nigerian Dried Cayenne Pepper](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nigerian_Dried_Cayenne_Pepper.jpg), Wikimedia Commons, Nigerian market photograph.
- `nigerian-egusi-bowl.jpg` — [Genesis Restaurant Egusi Soup, Victoria Island](https://chowdeck.com/store/vi/restaurants/genesis-restaurant-victoria-islandb21sbn), image served by Chowdeck for a Lagos restaurant listing.

The catalogue uses these images as Nigerian market reference photography. When
Koko Market has its own product photography, replace the local file while
keeping the same browser-safe `/images/<filename>` URL.
